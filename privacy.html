Our Privacy Policy

  1. Home
  2. Privacy Policy

Bridge Translation Services values your trust and is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information.


Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: Name, contact details, billing information, and project-related data provided by clients.
  • Project Content: Documents and files submitted for translation.
  • Technical Data: Website usage statistics, cookies, and analytics to improve user experience.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process translation requests and deliver services.
  • To communicate with clients regarding projects, quotes, and support.
  • To improve our services and website functionality.
  • To comply with legal and regulatory requirements.

Data Security

We employ strict security measures, including encryption and secure servers, to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. All staff and freelancers undergo background checks and are bound by confidentiality agreements.


Confidentiality

  • All client documents are treated with the highest level of confidentiality.
  • Sensitive content (legal, medical, financial, or technical) is handled only by qualified professionals.
  • We never share or sell client information to third parties.

Client Rights

  • You may request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • You may request corrections or deletion of your data, subject to legal and contractual obligations.
  • You may opt out of non-essential communications at any time.

Third-Party Services

Our website may use trusted third-party tools (e.g., analytics, payment processors). These providers have their own privacy policies, and we ensure they meet strict compliance standards.


Harmful or Sensitive Content

Clients must flag potentially sensitive or triggering material when submitting projects. This ensures safe handling by our staff and freelance community.


Policy Updates

Bridge may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on our website, and significant updates will be communicated directly to clients.
